# Product

## Register

product

## Users

Metin2 players using a desktop browser to scan market listings, compare prices,
track items, and configure targeted alerts. They need dense market information
to be fast to read and easy to filter.

## Product Purpose

Turn authorized market listing feeds into reliable price history, equipment
analysis, deal signals, and actionable searches. Success means a player can
move from a market question to a trustworthy result with minimal friction.

## Brand Personality

Quiet, practical, precise. The interface should feel like a focused utility,
not a game-themed promotional page.

## Anti-references

Avoid generic AI dashboard styling, decorative gradients, glass effects,
oversized cards, excessive pills, dark-by-default surfaces, and ornamental
game branding inside the working interface.

## Design Principles

- Put market data before decoration.
- Prefer familiar controls and compact, readable tables.
- Use hierarchy and whitespace instead of stacking cards.
- Keep copy short and operational.
- Make loading, empty, error, and selected states immediately understandable.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Use semantic controls, visible keyboard focus, readable contrast, non-color
state cues, and reduced-motion-safe interactions. Target WCAG 2.1 AA.
